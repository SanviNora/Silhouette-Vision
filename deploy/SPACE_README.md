# Silhouette Vision

Upload a fashion photo to find similar products, see predicted attributes, check whether it's an
exact match or a recognised luxury model, and understand why results match. A second tab
forecasts 12-week demand for a new product from look-alike past launches.

Code, methods and evaluation: https://github.com/SanviNora/Silhouette-Vision

Data: Fashion Product Images (Myntra, MIT); Visuelle 2.0 (Skenderi et al., CVPRW 2022,
CC BY-NC-SA 4.0). Portfolio project, not affiliated with any brand or retailer.
